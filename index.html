01 / ABOUT

Understanding infection.
Advancing protection.

I am a fifth-year PhD candidate in the School of Basic Medical Sciences at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Linqi Zhang.

My research interests span antibody discovery, vaccine design, infectious diseases, and AI for biology. I am affiliated with the Comprehensive AIDS Research Center and the Center for Infection Biology at Tsinghua Medicine.

Before my PhD, I earned a BSc in Biology from Lanzhou University in June 2022.

03 / EXPERIMENTAL EXPERTISE

Wet lab skills.

01

Monoclonal antibody isolation

Isolation of monoclonal antibodies from samples obtained from normal mice, humanized mice, alpacas, and humans. Techniques include single B-cell isolation, as well as the construction and screening of yeast surface display libraries.

02

High-throughput sequencing

Single-cell RNA-seq and BCR-seq.

03

Molecular biology & biochemistry

Western blotting, immunoprecipitation (IP), ELISA, immunohistochemistry, protein expression and purification, surface plasmon resonance (SPR), crystal screening, flow cytometry, and pseudovirus packaging.

04

Vaccine preparation

Protein-based vaccines, mRNA vaccines, and virus-like particles.

05

Animal manipulation

Immunization and infection, surgery, and intravital imaging.

06

Neuroscience

Brain stereotactic injection, optogenetics, calcium imaging, and patch clamp.
